Copilot Chat is available to Microsoft business users. The features you can use depend on whether your admin has assigned you a Microsoft Copilot add-on license.
Note
To use Copilot Chat you need to sign in with your Microsoft 365 account and have a qualifying Microsoft 365 business or enterprise subscription.
Copilot Chat with or without an add-on license
| Feature | Without a Microsoft Copilot license (Copilot Chat only) | With a Microsoft Copilot license |
|---|---|---|
| AI model access | Standard access | Priority access |
| Web data reasoning | Yes | Yes |
| Work data reasoning (Work IQ) | Limited (uploaded files) | Full (meetings, emails, chats, files, and more) |
| Access to agents | Limited (no advanced agents) | Extensive use (including advanced agents like Researcher, Analyst, and custom agents) |
| Security | Enterprise grade | Enterprise grade |
| Copilot studio access | Pay-as-you-go | Included |
| Priority | Standard (may be limited during peak times) | Priority (faster, more reliable) |
| Copilot in Outlook | Included | Included |
| Copilot in Microsoft 365 apps | Depends on your label | Included |
For additional information about what you have access to with Copilot sign into copilot.cloud.microsoft check what label is assigned to you.
A label of Copilot Chat (Basic), M365 Copilot (Basic), or M365 Copilot (Premium) appears under your name.
This label indicates where you can access Copilot directly. Learn more about labels and what they mean in What Copilot license do I have.
